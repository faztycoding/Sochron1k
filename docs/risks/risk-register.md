# Sochron1k Risk Register

This register records material risks for the Demo-first project. Controls listed here are requirements until retained evidence proves them effective. The project owner is the temporary owner for unassigned risks.

| ID | Risk and trigger | Impact | Tier | Required controls and evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-001 | A live account is selected through config, stale state, or adapter error | Unauthorized real-money execution | Critical consequence, High project control | Hard Demo preflight immediately before send; denied-live fixtures; no live path in the release artifact; explicit account/server evidence | Open; the source EA has no live/contest branch and rechecks exact Demo identity before its sole send call, but compilation and denied-live target evidence remain pending |
| R-002 | A send succeeds but the response is lost, then a retry creates another order | Duplicate exposure and invalid research data | High | Durable command ID and idempotency key; `UNKNOWN` state; MT5 reconciliation before retry; timeout-after-acceptance fault test | Open; API replay/no-replacement and a source-level terminal ledger, exact outcome replay and cumulative reconciliation now exist; actual lock/durability/restart/MT5 evidence remains pending |
| R-003 | A position fills without a confirmed broker-side SL | Loss can exceed the intended risk budget | High | Confirm SL from MT5; emergency-close runbook; alert; rejected-SL integration fixture; never label unconfirmed state protected | Open; source reconciliation reads current `POSITION_SL` and reports unprotected otherwise, but target rejected-SL emergency-close and alert evidence remain pending |

## Immediate priorities

SCN-001 must produce evidence for R-001 through R-007, R-010, and R-012 before any unattended Demo gate. R-008 and R-013 become blocking when strategy evaluation starts. R-009 and R-011 become blocking when AI and Supabase boundaries enter the implementation.

## Exception policy

A missing required control remains `BLOCKED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `FAIL`. It cannot be relabelled `PASS`. Any temporary exception must name the exact control, affected revision and environment, compensating control, authorized risk owner, expiry, restoration task, and retained approval record. The change author cannot accept a High or Critical risk on the owner's behalf.
